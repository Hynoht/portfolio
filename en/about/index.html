Who am I?

My name is Tolotra Anthony RANDRIAMBOLOLONA — Arthinez online. I’m a full-stack developer in Antananarivo, passionate about technology and everything around it.

I’m driven by curiosity, continuous learning and the ambition to contribute meaningfully to tomorrow’s digital world — including making it accessible to everyone through the training sessions I deliver.
